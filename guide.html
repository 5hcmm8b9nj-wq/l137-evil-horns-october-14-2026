💡 Always open the app the same way each time, either from the home screen icon or the same browser. That way your names and progress are always there.

2Find your way around

The buttons along the bottom

🏠Home: the start page with the big ▶ START PRAYING button.
🗣️Declare: all of Pastor Kiki's words, color-coded. Listen to one line, or mark a line as declared.
🔁Pray: choose a group of declarations and pray them hands-free.
👨‍👩‍👧Family: optional. The 3 Scriptures from the direction, read as written or spoken over your loved ones' names.
📖Learn: Understanding the Evil Horns, at Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced levels, with ▶ LISTEN buttons, quizzes and Scripture memory cards.

The buttons along the top

%The yellow circle shows how much you have completed. Tap it to see your progress.
AText size. Tap once for large, twice for extra large, three times to go back to normal.
🌙Day or night. Night mode is easier on the eyes in the dark or outside at night.
💡 Lost? Tap 🏠 Home at the bottom left. It always takes you back to the start.

3Listen hands-free

Before you start

  • Turn your phone's volume up using the buttons on the side.
  • Make sure your phone is not on silent. On iPhone, check the switch or Action button on the side.
  • Charge your phone, or keep it plugged in.
  • Set your screen to stay on. See step 4.

Choose how you want to pray

On the Home page, under "How do you want to pray?", tap one:

🎧Listen only: reads everything straight through with no pauses.
🗣️Repeat after me: says "Repeat after me", reads the line, says "Your turn", waits while you declare, then carries on by itself.
📖Study mode: reads one line, opens the Bible study under it, then waits. Tap ⏭ Forward when you are ready for the next line.

You can also change this later: tap ⚙ Voice on the black player bar.

Start

  1. Go to 🏠 Home.
  2. Tap the big ▶ START PRAYING button once.
  3. In Listen only or Repeat after me, set the phone down and pray. You do not need to touch it again.

What you will hear

  1. The 4 instructions, then the 3 Scriptures, then all 21 declarations, in Pastor Kiki's order and in her exact words.
  2. Then her closing direction, "NOW POUR THE BLOOD OF JESUS ON THE EARTH AND THANK THE LORD," and her blessing.

The line being read lights up in gold, and the black bar shows its number, for example "12 of 30 · Declaration 5 of 21". The gold progress bar shows how many of the 21 declarations you have completed.

The player buttons (black bar at the bottom)

⏮Back: go back to the line before.
▶ ⏸Play / Pause: tap to pause. Tap again to carry on from the same line.
↺Replay: read the current line again.
⏹Stop: stops and goes back to the beginning.
⏭Forward: skip to the next line.
⚙Voice: opens the settings below.

Voice settings (tap ⚙ Voice)

  • How do you want to pray? Listen only, Repeat after me, or Study.
  • Your-turn time: how long you get to declare: Auto, 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 seconds. Auto gives more time for longer lines.
  • Speed: choose 0.75× or 0.9× if the voice is too fast, or 1.25× or 1.5× to go faster.
  • Voice: pick a different reading voice. Tap 🔈 Test the voice to hear it.
  • Tap ⚙ Voice again to close the settings.

Start from any line

  • Go to 🗣️ Declare and use "Or start from any line" to pick a line from the list.
  • Or, on any card, tap ▶ Listen from here. It carries on from that line.
  • Tap 🔊 This line only to hear just one line, for example one Scripture.

Resume where you left off

The app remembers the last line you reached. On the Home page, tap the yellow ▶ Resume where I left off button. It also appears in My Progress.

4Keep the screen on so it reads non-stop

⚠️ Important: this app reads aloud using your phone's built-in voice inside the web browser. Phone browsers do not keep this voice playing when the screen locks, and it pauses if you switch to another app. Playing with the screen locked is not supported. To pray non-stop, the screen must stay on.

While it is reading, the app asks your phone to keep the screen on. Some phones allow this and some do not, so change this setting once to be sure:

  1. Open Settings, the grey gear.
  2. Tap Display & Brightness.
  3. Tap Auto-Lock.
  4. Choose Never, or the longest time listed.
  5. Go back to the app.
🔋 If Auto-Lock is grey and you cannot change it, Low Power Mode is on. Turn it off in Settings → Battery → Low Power Mode, then try again.
  1. Open Settings, the gear.
  2. Tap Display.
  3. Tap Screen timeout. Some phones call it Sleep.
  4. Choose the longest time listed, for example 10 minutes.
  5. Go back to the app.
💡 Can't find it? Open Settings, tap the 🔍 search at the top, and type timeout.
🔋 Power saving mode can turn the screen off sooner. Turn it off while you pray.

While it is reading

  • Do not press the side button. That locks the screen and stops the voice.
  • Stay in the app. If you open WhatsApp or another app, the reading pauses.
  • When you come back, a yellow bar says "Paused while you were away". Tap ▶ RESUME and it carries on from the same line.
  • Put the phone face up and plugged in if you can.
💡 When you have finished praying, you can set Auto-Lock or Screen timeout back to how it was, to save battery.

5My Family Prayers and your loved ones' names

This part is optional and is not part of Pastor Kiki's direction. Her 21 declarations are never changed. Tap 👨‍👩‍👧 Family at the bottom. Choose how to read:

📜Read the original Scripture: Psalm 75:10, Psalm 3:3 and Revelation 12:11 exactly as written in the King James Bible.
🙏🏽Personalized prayer: each verse is shown first, then spoken over the names you add. It is in a grey dotted box, because it is not a direct Bible quotation.

Add names

  1. Tap 🙏🏽 Personalized prayer.
  2. Tap the box that says Add a name and type one name, for example your own.
  3. Tap Add. Do the same for your spouse, children, parents and loved ones. Or type several with commas.
  4. To fix a spelling, tap the × on the name and add it again.

Tap ▶ Play all 3, ▶ Listen from here on any verse, or 🔊 This verse only.

🔒 Protecting your names

  • Names are saved only in this phone's browser. They are not sent to anyone.
  • 📤 Share the app (no names) shares the plain link. Use this for church, WhatsApp groups and friends.
  • 🔗 Copy my family link (with names) puts the names inside the link. The app warns you first. Only send it to your own family.

6Follow Pastor Kiki's direction with the app

Pastor Kiki's four instructions, in her own words. The app reads them in this same order, then the 3 Scriptures, the 21 declarations, her closing direction and her blessing:

    💡 App tip: outside, headphones or a small Bluetooth speaker make the voice easier to hear. If it is dark, tap 🌙 for night mode.

    7What the colors and boxes mean

    The colors never change. They tell you what kind of line you are reading:

    Purple: Pastor Kiki's instructions.
    Royal blue: Scripture.
    Orange-red: the declarations where Pastor Kiki's own words mention pouring: 1, 3, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17 and 20.
    Black: all the other declarations. In night mode these show in white.

    Two kinds of boxes

    Blue frame: Pastor Kiki's original prophetic direction, or the Bible text, exactly as given.
    Grey dotted box: Supplementary Biblical Study. Extra explanation to help you understand. It is not part of Pastor Kiki's original direction.
    💡 There is nothing to set up. The colors are already done for you.

    8Pray a Group, Learn and Progress

    🔁 Pray a Group

    1. Tap 🔁 Pray.
    2. Choose what to pray, for example “As I pour” declarations or Scriptures only.
    3. Tap START PRAYING HANDS-FREE. Each line fills the screen, one at a time.

    📖 Learn

    • Choose 🌱 Beginner, 🌿 Intermediate or 🌳 Advanced. Each level teaches from the King James Bible.
    • Answer the questions at the end of each level. The answer appears after you choose.
    • Everything on the Learn page is Supplementary Biblical Study, in a grey dotted box. It is not part of Pastor Kiki's original direction.
    • On any card, tap 💡 Understand to see what that line means at each level.

    🏆 Progress

    Tap the yellow % circle at the top. You will see the line you reached, how many declarations you prayed this time, how many are left, and which prayer groups you have finished.

    • When a line has been read and your "Your turn" has finished, it is marked ✔ Declared.
    • You can also tap ✔ I declared it yourself.
    • Tap a number under "Declarations still to pray" to go straight to that declaration.
    • To clear everything and begin again, tap Start again at the bottom of My Progress, then OK. Your names stay saved.
    • Progress only helps you keep your place. It is not a measure of spiritual power, holiness or deliverance.

    9Helpful tips if you are new to apps

    • Tap gently, once. Wait a second for the app to respond before tapping again.
    • Scroll by sliding your finger up the screen to see more.
    • Make the words bigger with the A button at the top.
    • If something looks wrong, tap 🏠 Home or close the page and open the link again.
    • You need the internet to open the app. Wi-Fi or mobile data both work.
    • Keep the app on your home screen (step 1) so you don't need to find the link each time.
    • Don't clear your browser history if you want to keep your names and progress. If you do, just add your names again or open your family link.
    • Ask someone to sit with you the first time. After one practice it becomes easy.
    • Practise indoors first with one line: go to 🗣️ Declare, tap ▶ Listen on declaration 1, then tap ⏹ Stop.

    10If something is not working

    🔇I hear nothing. Turn the volume up and take the phone off silent. Tap ⚙ Voice, then 🔈 Test the voice. If you opened the link from WhatsApp or Facebook, tap the menu (⋯ or ⋮) and choose Open in Safari or Open in Chrome.
    ⏸It stops after one line. You may be in Study mode. Tap ⚙ Voice and choose Repeat after me or Listen only.
    🌑It stops when the screen goes dark. Follow step 4 to keep the screen on, then tap ▶ RESUME or ▶ Play.
    🐇The voice is too fast. Tap ⚙ Voice, then Speed 0.75× or 0.9×.
    ⏳Not enough time to declare. Tap ⚙ Voice, then Your-turn time 20 s or 30 s.
    🔁I want to hear a line again. Tap ↺ Replay on the black bar.
    👤A name is spelled wrong. In 🌾 Deut 28 (Personalized prayer), tap × on the name and add it again.
    🔤The voice says a name strangely. Try typing it the way it sounds, or pick another voice in ⚙ Voice.
    🔄The screen looks stuck. Pull the page down to refresh, or close and reopen the link. Your names and progress stay saved.
